ABOUT MEDIKIOSK

Making Patient Information More Organized

MediKiosk is designed to simplify the initial patient case-taking process. It helps collect health information, identify selected warning symptoms and prepare a structured summary for doctor review.

Our aim is to make the first step of a healthcare consultation more organized and accessible.
